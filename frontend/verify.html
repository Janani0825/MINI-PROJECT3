Verify my website

Own a genuine website (college, shop, clinic, portfolio...)? Ask the administrator to verify it. Once approved, anyone who scans your address sees that it is genuine, what it is used for and who owns it.

Request verification
How it works
  1. Send the request with your proof.
  2. You get a verification code. Put it in your website (a line in the page, or a file named /.well-known/phishguard.txt) so the admin can see the site is really yours.
  3. The administrator checks the category, evidence, e-mail and automatic risk checks, then approves or rejects.
  4. After approval, scans of your address show a Verified website card.
